Perseverance Sets All-Time Off-World Driving Record

NASA's Perseverance rover has shattered the all-time off-world driving record, traveling farther across another world than any previous vehicle. Driven in part by an advanced self-driving system, Perseverance surpassed the prior 28.06-mile record set by the Opportunity rover. While Opportunity accumulated that distance over nearly 15 years, Perseverance reached the milestone in five and a half years. Currently, Perseverance is exploring some of the oldest rocks in the solar system to search for clues regarding ancient Martian environments that might have once supported life.

Curiosity Observes Mysterious Wind-Carved Cliffs

NASA's Curiosity rover has captured a spectacular new perspective of Mars featuring wind-carved cliffs that may hold evidence of past volcanic activity. The rover is currently approaching these unusual landscape features, and scientists plan to investigate the formations to uncover the secrets behind their distinctive appearance in 2027.
