NASA updates

NASA has released several updates concerning its ongoing missions and operational activities. These include progress on the Artemis III mission, coverage of an International Space Station resupply launch, an update on its grant payment system, and its role in monitoring wildfire smoke events.

Artemis III Mission Progress

According to Ars Technica's "Rocket Report," engines have been installed for NASA's Artemis III mission.

International Space Station Resupply Mission

NASA announced it will provide live coverage for the launch and docking of the Roscosmos Progress 96 cargo spacecraft. This unpiloted resupply mission is scheduled to launch on Wednesday, Sept. 9, at 12:15 p.m. EDT (9:15 p.m. Baikonur time). The spacecraft will carry approximately three tons of food, fuel, and supplies for the crew aboard the International Space Station, as reported by NASA.

NASA Grant System Update

NASA has issued a notice regarding issues with the Payment Management System (PMS) affecting Federal Financial Report (FFR) generation for grant recipients. NASA states that impacted grant recipients will not be considered noncompliant and that the agency is actively working with PMS to resolve the issue. Updates on this situation will be posted on NASA's grants page.

Wildfire Smoke Monitoring

NASA Earthdata reported on dense smoke from wildland fires in Ontario, utilizing data from the Ozone Mapping and Profiler Suite (OMPS) on the Suomi National Polar orbiting Partnership (Suomi NPP) satellite. Researchers from NASA's Langley Research Center also provided a research summary of a similar wildfire smoke event from June 2023. Furthermore, NASA is a partner in AirNow, which provides updates and alerts on fire-related air quality in the U.S.
