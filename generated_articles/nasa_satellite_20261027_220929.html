Anak Krakatau Volcanic Eruption

In early September 2026, the Anak Krakatau volcano located between Java and Sumatra in Indonesia experienced an explosive eruption. On September 5, 2026, the VIIRS instrument aboard the NOAA-20 satellite detected steam, volcanic gas, and a brown ash cloud. Aerosol index values measured between 10 and 20 or greater, confirming high concentrations of suspended ash in the atmosphere. According to the Indonesian meteorological agency, volcanic ash reached altitudes up to 20,000 feet to the east and 50,000 feet to the west. The event grounded regional airline flights, degraded air quality in nearby areas including Jakarta, and injected sulfur dioxide into the upper troposphere and stratosphere at altitudes around 17 kilometers.
