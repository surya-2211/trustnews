NASA announced it would provide live coverage for the launch and docking of the unpiloted Roscosmos Progress 96 resupply spacecraft. Update on NASA Grant System Issues

NASA has issued a notice regarding issues with the Payment Management System (PMS), which is affecting Federal Financial Report (FFR) generation. NASA stated that impacted grant recipients will not be considered noncompliant and confirmed it is actively working with PMS to resolve the issue, with updates to be posted on its grants page.

NASA's Role in Wildland Fire Smoke Monitoring

NASA Earthdata reported on dense smoke from wildland fires in Ontario, noting that researchers from NASA's Langley Research Center provided a research summary of a 2023 wildfire smoke event. NASA is also a partner in AirNow, which provides updates and alerts on fire-related air quality in the U.S., alongside the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, NOAA, and other agencies.
