Google's AI weather model has received an update that significantly improves forecast accuracy, as reported by Science - Ars Technica. AI for Designing New Materials

AI is now assisting in the design of new materials that are practical for real-world applications, according to MIT News. Researchers at MIT have developed a tool called "CrysVCD." This innovation is expected to drastically reduce the time and financial resources typically spent on identifying and screening out chemically unstable designs in material development.

Enhancing Safety in Self-Driving Cars

A new system developed at MIT helps humans predict when self-driving cars might make mistakes, MIT News reported. This method, known as CW-Net, translates the complex reasoning processes of an autonomous vehicle's AI system into concepts that are understandable to humans, thereby explaining the vehicle's behavior.

Expanding AI Education Across Disciplines

The MIT Schwarzman College of Computing has launched a pilot program aimed at helping educators teach AI across various disciplines, according to MIT News. This initiative included a weeklong summer workshop that brought higher education faculty to campus to explore how AI and machine learning materials can be adapted and integrated into their respective classrooms.
