How is GPT-6 Astra different from GPT-6.1 Sol?

OpenAI has introduced two advanced models, GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol, each designed for distinct applications and performance profiles. While both offer high-level intelligence, they differ significantly in their capabilities, cost-effectiveness, and specific areas of expertise.

GPT-6 Astra: State-of-the-Art Intelligence and Security

GPT-6 Astra is characterized as OpenAI's most capable broadly deployed model and its most intelligent and aligned model to date. It boasts state-of-the-art capabilities across various domains, including computer use, coding, cybersecurity, and science. Notably, GPT-6 Astra is the first OpenAI model to achieve the Critical level of cybersecurity capability under the company's Preparedness Framework, highlighting its advanced security features and robustness.

GPT-6.1 Sol: Near-Astra Performance at Reduced Cost

GPT-6.1 Sol is positioned as a model offering 'near-Astra intelligence.' It is designed for tasks such as coding, computer use, and professional work. A key differentiator for GPT-6.1 Sol is its cost-efficiency, being available at one-fifth of Astra’s standard API input and output token prices, making high-level AI capabilities more accessible.

Key Differences in Capability and Cost

The primary distinction between the two models lies in their peak intelligence, specialized capabilities, and pricing. GPT-6 Astra represents the pinnacle of OpenAI's current broadly deployed models, offering the highest level of intelligence and state-of-the-art performance, particularly in critical areas like cybersecurity and science. GPT-6.1 Sol, while highly capable with 'near-Astra intelligence,' provides a more cost-effective solution for general professional work, coding, and computer use, without reaching the absolute highest tier of capability or the specific critical cybersecurity rating of Astra.
